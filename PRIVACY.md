# Privacy Policy — OnlyFails

**Last updated:** November 26, 2026

OnlyFails is a personal, automated content-curation tool used exclusively by its owner to publish YouTube Shorts to the OnlyFails YouTube channel.

## 1. Information We Access
When authorized by the channel owner via Google OAuth 2.0, the application requests only the minimum scope required to upload videos:
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload` — Used solely to upload rendered Short videos and their metadata (title, description, tags, category, and privacy setting) to the authorized YouTube channel.

## 2. How Information Is Used
- OAuth credentials (`YT_CLIENT_ID`, `YT_CLIENT_SECRET_VALUE`, and `YT_REFRESH_TOKEN`) are stored privately in the owner's local environment or encrypted GitHub Actions repository secrets.
- No personal user data, viewer data, or third-party Google account data is collected, stored, sold, or shared with any external party.

## 3. Data Retention & Revocation
- The owner may revoke the application's access to their Google/YouTube account at any time via [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## 4. Contact
For questions regarding this application, contact the repository owner via [GitHub](https://github.com/MohdSajidJafri/OnlyFails).
